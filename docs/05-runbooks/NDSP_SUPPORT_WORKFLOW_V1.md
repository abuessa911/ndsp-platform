# NDSP Support Workflow V1

## Official contact
support@ndsp.app

## Intake and ownership
Every request receives a unique case reference, owner, severity, status, and
last-update timestamp. Security and privacy complaints are never handled only
through informal chat.

## Response targets
- P1: service outage, account takeover, or material data exposure — acknowledge
  within 15 minutes and provide updates at least every 30 minutes.
- P2: payment, subscription, access, or major decision-room failure — acknowledge
  within 2 business hours.
- P3: normal product defect or account request — acknowledge within 1 business day.
- P4: question or improvement request — acknowledge within 2 business days.

## Escalation
P1 goes immediately to Owner, Security, and Operations. Payment disputes go to
Billing and Owner. Privacy complaints go to the designated privacy/legal owner.
Unresolved P2 cases escalate after 4 business hours.

## Incident communication
Communications state impact, affected scope, workarounds, next update time, and
resolution. Sensitive implementation details and user data are excluded.

## Complaint and refund handling
The case records the applicable terms, transaction reference, evidence,
decision, approver, and communication to the user. Refunds follow the approved
subscription and cancellation policy.

## Closure evidence
A case closes only after resolution, user communication, root-cause category,
and any prevention action are recorded.
