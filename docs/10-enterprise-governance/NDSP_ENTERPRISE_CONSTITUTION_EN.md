# NDSP Enterprise Constitution

NDSP — Nawaf Decision Support Platform is a governed decision-support and explainability platform. It is not a trading venue, execution engine, or buy/sell recommendation system.

Core rules:
1. One governed source of truth.
2. Decision layers are limited to NDSP-CORE-L01 through NDSP-CORE-L16.
3. Platform capabilities are not decision layers.
4. Completed decisions fail closed when data quality or CFTC validity fails.
5. Explainability, lineage, and governance are mandatory.
6. Access, billing, and presentation never change scientific decision value.
7. Simulation and replay never publish live decisions.
8. Every change must be testable, auditable, and reversible.
